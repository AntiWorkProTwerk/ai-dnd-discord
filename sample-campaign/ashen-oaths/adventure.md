# The Kiln at Varrow

A short adventure for 1-4 Sworn wanderers, about one session long.

# Starting Situation

Dusk, Day 12 of Frost. The characters have just been let through the gate of **Garrow's Rest** and sworn guest-peace before the Common Fire. Before they can eat, **Thane Ysolde Kett** asks them to stand before the fire again. Her son **Edric**, sixteen, left three nights ago with two friends to "see the lights at Varrow Kiln." The friends came back without him, babbling about a fire that sang. The Thane cannot spare spears with ash-wolves at the walls.

**The offer:** bring Edric home. The Thane offers 4 supply each, a winter's shelter in the Rest, and her own bond. She asks the characters to **Swear an Oath** before the Common Fire to do it.

# Scene 1: The Common Fire

The characters can question **Wenna**, one of the friends who came back. She is `shaken` and frightened of the Thane. A Parley gets the story: three strangers in grey clay masks welcomed the boys at the kiln and offered Edric "a crown of fire." Edric went willingly. Wenna ran.

**Read the Ash** on Wenna or the Thane can reveal that the Thane is more afraid than angry, and that she glances at a locked chest by the fire whenever Varrow is mentioned.

# Scene 2: The Road East

Half a day across ash plains and the edge of the Blackwood. Have the party Face Danger or Resupply along the way. On a miss, **three ash-wolves** (harm max 2 each, harm 2 bites, tags: pack, fear-fire) shadow the party, attacking at dusk or when someone strays. The **Ash Pilgrims** camp at the ford. Their elder, **Mother Sabel**, will share food (1 supply) and warns: "The kiln is lit again. Nothing good is fired twice."

# Scene 3: Varrow Kiln

The great kiln is a brick dome as wide as a hall, its mouth glowing orange. Around it, the village is shells and chimneys. Inside, hundreds of clay **oath-tablets** are stacked on racks, each stamped with old lords' names. Three **Kiln-Born** in clay masks tend the fire. Their leader is **Brother Cinder** (harm max 4, harm 2 with a burning brand, tags: fanatic, embercraft). **Edric** sits beside the fire, unhurt, holding a blank tablet.

The Kiln-Born are not cruel. They believe that firing a new oath with the blood of a ruling house will relight the High Hearth. Characters can:

- **Parley** with Brother Cinder: on 10+ he agrees to let Edric choose freely; on 7-9 he demands a Sworn's oath in exchange.
- **Clash** with the Kiln-Born (the other two have harm max 2 and deal harm 1).
- **Read the Ash** to understand the ritual: Edric's blood on the tablet, fired at dawn.
- Use **Embercraft** or Face Danger +Iron to collapse the kiln's flue, which ends the ritual for good but sets the racks ablaze (harm 3 to anyone still inside).

# Resolution

If Edric returns, the Thane pays and honors the oath (each character marks 2 xp for fulfilling it). If the characters learn the secret below, they face a harder choice about what to tell Edric, the Thane, or the Rest.

# GM Only: The Secret

**Do not reveal this to players unless they discover it in play.**

Edric is not the Thane's son by blood. He is the last surviving child of the old kingdom's royal house, smuggled out of the burning capital as an infant. Ysolde Kett swore an oath to the dying queen to raise him hidden. The locked chest by the Common Fire holds the queen's half-burned oath-tablet and Edric's birth seal.

The Kiln-Born found out from an Ash Pilgrim who once carried the infant. Their ritual needs royal blood, and it might even work. Brother Cinder honestly believes it will. If the ritual is completed, the GM decides what wakes. It will not be the kingdom that was.

Edric does not know. Telling him or the Rest would break the Thane's oath, and the Thane would become `forsworn`.

# Non-Player Characters

- **Thane Ysolde Kett:** stern, tired, fiercely protective. Harm max 6. Tags: leader, spear. Attitude: hopeful but guarded.
- **Edric Kett:** restless, romantic, wants to matter. Harm max 4. Attitude: defiant.
- **Wenna:** Edric's friend, guilty and frightened. Harm max 3.
- **Mother Sabel:** Ash Pilgrim elder, gentle, knows old roads and old secrets. Harm max 3.
- **Brother Cinder:** soft-spoken Kiln-Born leader with a ruined voice. Harm max 4, harm 2. Tags: fanatic, embercraft.
