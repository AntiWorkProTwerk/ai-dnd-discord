# The Cinderlands

Thirty winters ago the High Hearth broke. The great oath-fire that bound the old kingdom's lords to their word went out, and in the following year every promise sealed by its flame turned to fire. Castles burned from inside. Bridges, built under oath, collapsed. The land between the Grey Teeth mountains and the cold sea is now the **Cinderlands**: black forests, ash plains, and a few stubborn holdfasts.

People still swear oaths. They do it carefully, before small fires, and the ash of those fires carries a little power. Wanderers who keep their oaths are called **the Sworn**, and holdfasts welcome them, warily.

## Garrow's Rest

A walled holdfast of about two hundred people built inside the shell of a burned abbey, on a hill above the Ashwater river. The chieftain is **Thane Ysolde Kett**, a scarred, practical woman who keeps the gate shut after dark. The **Common Fire** burns in the old nave, and every newcomer must swear guest-peace before it: harm no one inside the walls, and take nothing unshared.

Trade is done in **supply**: smoked fish, lamp tallow, arrows, salt. Coins from the old kingdom are melted for nails.

## Nearby

- **The Ashwater Ford:** the only safe crossing for a day in either direction, watched by a ruined toll tower.
- **Varrow Kiln:** a dead village half a day east, built around an enormous brick kiln where the old kingdom fired its oath-tablets. Scavengers avoid it. Lights have been seen there.
- **The Blackwood:** a burned forest whose trees still smolder underground. Ash-wolves den here.

## Factions

- **The Hold of Garrow's Rest:** Thane Kett and her twenty spears. They want safety, salt, and to survive the winter.
- **The Kiln-Born:** a rumored band who claim the old oaths can be restored if the right tablets are fired again.
- **The Ash Pilgrims:** wandering mourners who walk from ruin to ruin, burying what they find. They are harmless, and they know every road.

## Time

Days are counted from the first frost: "Day 12 of Frost." Locals divide the day into **grey dawn, day, dusk, and dark**. Winter is coming, so dark falls early, and nobody travels in it by choice. The adventure begins at dusk on Day 12 of Frost.
