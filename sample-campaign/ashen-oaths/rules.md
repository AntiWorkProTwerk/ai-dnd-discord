# Ashen Oaths Rules

Ashen Oaths is a short, fiction-first ruleset about sworn wanderers crossing a burned land. It uses two six-sided dice, five stats, a harm track instead of health points, and a shared idea of **supply** instead of coins. There is no armor class and no initiative order. Original sample material.

# The Roll

When a character does something risky, the player rolls **2d6 + the relevant stat** (stats range from -1 to +3).

- **10 or more: full success.** You do it, and it goes the way you wanted.
- **7 to 9: partial success.** You do it, but there is a cost, a complication, or a hard choice. The GM offers the cost, or two options to choose between.
- **6 or less: failure.** Things go wrong. The GM makes a move (see GM Moves), and the character marks 1 xp.

Only roll when the outcome is uncertain and failure is interesting. Never roll twice for the same attempt unless the situation has changed.

**Edge and burden.** A strong position (high ground, surprise, the right tool) gives **+1** to the roll. A bad position (wounded, outnumbered, blind) gives **-1**. Bonuses do not stack beyond +2 or -2 total.

**Aid.** An ally who helps describes how, and the helper's player rolls +Heart. On 10+, the acting character takes +1. On 7-9, they take +1 but the helper shares any consequence.

# Stats

- **Iron:** force, endurance, fighting up close, holding a door.
- **Edge:** speed, aim, archery, quick hands.
- **Heart:** courage, leadership, persuasion, keeping oaths.
- **Shadow:** stealth, deception, misdirection.
- **Wits:** noticing, knowing, tracking, crafting, embercraft.

# Moves

Moves are what characters do. Narrate the action first; the GM names the move if a roll is needed.

**Face Danger** (stat that fits): act despite a threat or under pressure. On 7-9, you stumble, hesitate, or pay for it: take 1 harm, lose 1 supply, or leave something behind (GM's choice).

**Clash** (+Iron, or +Edge at range): fight an enemy within reach. On 10+, inflict harm and choose one: take no harm in return, or inflict +1 harm. On 7-9, inflict harm and take the enemy's harm in return. On 6-, the GM makes a move, usually harming you.

**Parley** (+Heart, or +Shadow if lying): press someone to do what you want with leverage or an appeal. On 10+, they do it. On 7-9, they want something first, or do it badly. A promise made in a Parley can become an oath.

**Read the Ash** (+Wits): study a place or person. On 10+, ask the GM three questions; on 7-9, ask one. Questions: What happened here? What is hidden? Who is in control? What is the safest way through? What are they really after? Take +1 when acting on an answer.

**Make Camp** (no roll; costs 1 supply for the whole party): rest for a night. Each character heals 1 harm. Without spending supply, no one heals and everyone takes the **hungry** condition.

**Resupply** (+Wits): forage, scavenge, or trade. On 10+, gain 2 supply. On 7-9, gain 1 supply, or 2 with a complication (someone saw you, you owe a favor). In a settlement you may trade an item of value for supply at the GM's ruling.

**Mend** (+Wits, costs 1 supply): tend a companion's wounds. On 10+, heal 2 harm. On 7-9, heal 1 harm. Each character can be mended once per scene.

**Embercraft** (+Wits, costs 1 supply): call on the ash-bound magic of an oath. Name the effect: a flame that will not go out, a voice heard across a valley, a ward on a door until dawn, warmth for the night, a glimpse of a dead person's last moment. On 10+, it works. On 7-9, it works, but choose one: take 1 harm, the effect is weaker or brief, or something hears you. On 6-, the ember turns: the GM chooses a backlash.

**Swear an Oath** (+Heart): swear to do something difficult. Name it, and to whom. On 10+, take +1 forward on your first roll toward it. On 7-9, you are not sure you can manage it; the GM reveals an obstacle. When you fulfill an oath, mark 2 xp. When you break one, take the **forsworn** condition until you make amends.

**Last Stand** (+Heart): when your harm reaches its maximum, roll. On 10+, you stay on your feet; set harm to its maximum minus 1, and you must make camp or be mended before you can fight again. On 7-9, you survive but are out of the scene, and you gain a lasting scar (a condition such as "scarred: bad leg"). On 6-, you are dying; unless an ally reaches you this scene with a Mend at 10+, you die, or the GM offers a terrible bargain instead.

# Harm

Every character and dangerous NPC has a **harm track from 0 to 6**. Harm starts at 0 and goes up. It can never go below 0 or above the maximum.

The GM sets harm by the danger: **1** for a scrape, a punch, a fall, cold; **2** for a blade, an arrow, a beast's bite; **3** for a crushing blow, fire, or a monster.

**Armor** is not a number. A character with an armored item, such as a scale coat or a heavy shield, reduces each harm they take by 1 (to a minimum of 1), but has -1 on Face Danger rolls involving speed or stealth.

NPCs use the same track. Minor foes usually have a maximum of 2 or 3. When an NPC's harm reaches its maximum, it is defeated: dead, fled, or surrendered, as fits the fiction.

# Supply

**Supply** is an integer from 0 upward that stands for food, lamp oil, bandages, arrows, and small trade goods. Each character starts with **3 supply**. Moves above spend it. Characters can give supply to one another freely. At 0 supply, a character can't Mend or use Embercraft and gains **hungry** at the next camp.

# Conditions

Conditions are lowercase labels: **hungry** (-1 on all rolls until you eat), **shaken** (-1 on Heart rolls until a quiet scene), **forsworn** (-1 on Heart rolls; NPCs who know distrust you), **burned** (1 harm cannot be healed until Make Camp twice), and **scars**, which are permanent and descriptive.

# GM Moves

On a failure, or when players look to the GM, choose one: inflict harm; take away supply; separate them; reveal an unwelcome truth; show signs of a threat that is getting closer; put someone in danger; offer an opportunity with a cost; turn their move back on them; advance a hidden plan. Always follow with "What do you do?"

# Character Creation

1. **Name and look.** Pick a name and one distinctive feature, such as soot-grey eyes or a burned hand.
2. **Path.** Choose one path:
   - **Blade:** a sworn guard. Talent: *Shieldwall*, which lets you take harm meant for an adjacent ally. Starts with a longblade and scale coat (armored).
   - **Shade:** a scout and thief. Talent: *Gone*, which gives +1 when you Face Danger to escape or hide. Starts with a bow and quiver and a dark cloak.
   - **Speaker:** an envoy and oath-witness. Talent: *Binding Word*, which makes an NPC who swears an oath before you keep it unless you break faith first. Starts with a herald's staff and a sealed letter of passage.
   - **Ember-Keeper:** a tender of the oath-fires. Talent: *Steady Flame*, which allows you to roll Embercraft without spending supply once per day. Starts with an ember lantern and a bag of ash-salt.
3. **Stats.** Assign **+2, +1, +1, 0, -1** among Iron, Edge, Heart, Shadow, Wits.
4. **Harm and supply.** Harm 0 of max 6. Supply 3.
5. **Bonds.** Write one bond with each other player character, such as "Rook pulled me from the fire," and one with an NPC.
6. **Oath.** Swear one personal oath. It does not need a roll at creation.

# Advancement

Mark xp when you roll a 6 or less (1 xp) and when you fulfill an oath (2 xp). At **5 xp**, reset to 0 and choose one: +1 to a stat (maximum +3), a new talent from another path, or +1 starting supply after each Make Camp.

# Field Conventions

- **Characters and NPCs (data):** `path`, `stats` {`iron`, `edge`, `heart`, `shadow`, `wits`} (integers -1 to +3), `harm` {`current`, `max`} (current 0 to max, max normally 6), `supply` (integer, 0 or more), `bonds` [strings], `oaths` [strings], `talents` [strings], `conditions` [lowercase strings], `xp` (integer). NPCs may use only `harm`, `tags` [strings] and `attitude`. There are **no coins, no HP, and no armor class**. Do not add them.
- **Items (data):** `quantity` (integer, 0 or more), `description`, `tags` (for example ["armored"], ["ranged"]). Inventory is derived from parent records.
- **Locations (data):** `description`, `exits` [{`to_id`, `label`}].
- **Scenes (data):** `status` ("active" or "ended"), `participants_ids`, `initiative` stays [] (spotlight passes by fiction), `turn_of_id` (whose spotlight it is, or null), `round` (0), `time` (e.g. "Day 1, dusk"), `pending_decision`.
- **Quests (data):** `status` ("open", "completed", or "failed"), `objectives` [strings], `giver_id`, `reward`. A sworn oath to an NPC is usually also a quest.
